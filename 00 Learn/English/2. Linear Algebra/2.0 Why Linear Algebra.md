# Why Linear Algebra
Linear Algebra is a mathematical language used to describe Quantum Mechanics. Quantum Mechanics will be a bunch of messy integrations and Fourier transforms without linear algebra. Besides Quantum Mechanics, Linear Algebra appears in our daily life: Artificial Intelligence, image processing, etc.

Even some of you might not have heard of them before, but you definitely studied some of them (vectors, matrices) in high school. If you already forgot or never studied yet, don’t worry; we will guide you from scratch.

In this module will guide you **some** basic linear algebra concepts to understand basic of Quantum Computing which includes:
- 2.1 Vector
- 2.2 Matrices
- 2.3 Basis
- 2.4 Orthogonal
- 2.5 Orthonormal basis
- 2.6 Complex number
- 2.7 Eigenvalues and Eigenvectors
- 2.8 Special Operators
- 2.9 Tensor Product
- 2.10 Dirac Notation
