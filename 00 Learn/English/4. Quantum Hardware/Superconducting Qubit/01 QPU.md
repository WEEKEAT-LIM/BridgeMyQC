## Review on Natural Atom: Bohr's Model
![Bohr's Model](./assets/Bohr1.png)

The electrons are orbiting the nucleus, and carrying different amount of energy in each level. Electron from ground state can promote to excited state when specific amount of energy ($\Delta E$) is transferred to it. Generally, we shoot a photon(electromagnetic wave) with frequency of $\nu$ to transfer energy to system, because $\Delta E = h \nu$. Which means $\Delta E$ must exactly be $E_{target} - E_{current}$. So, the photon with $E_{a}$ or $E_{b}$ not able to excite electron from n1 to any state(level).
