## DiVincenzo's criteria
If a device meet these conditions, it can perform quantum computation:
1. A scalable physical system with well-characterized qubits
2. The ability to initialize the state of qubits
3. Long relevant quantum coherence times
4. A "universal" set of quantum gates
5. A qubit-specific measurement capability

## General Benchmarks
- `$T_1`(Decoherence time): Lifetime of qubit, time take from $\ket{1}$ to $\ket{0}$ naturally
- `$T_2$` (Coherence time): Lifetime of superposition state
- Single-qubit gate fidelity: How closely a single quantum gate operation to physical qubit matches its ideal
- Two-qubit gate fidelity: How closely a two quantum gate operation to physical qubits matches its ideal
- Readout fidelity: The probability of readout apparatus identifies the state of qubit correctly
- Crosstalk error: Unwanted interactions between physical qubits that occur when perform operation(disturb its neighbor )
